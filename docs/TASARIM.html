03 · Atmosphere

Glow & motion

Effects are the brand. Each has one job, and the timings below are the ones the game ships with.

NEON
neon-cyan

Three stacked shadows: 6, 24, 70px. Titles and the active screen header.

NEON
neon-red

Same stack in pink, with an orange outer halo for heat. Only on the second logo word.

FLICKER
anim-flicker · 4s

Two short dips per cycle, like a tired tube. Logo only.

▶
anim-blink · 1.1s step-end

Hard on/off to 15%. Cursor arrows and selection rings.

anim-bob · 2.6s

10px float. Fighter previews on the select screen.

grid-floor · 2.4s

44px cyan grid, 58° tilt, scrolls toward the player.

scanlines

1px dark line every 3px, laid over every full screen (z 30).

Focus glow

0 0 28px rgba(0,240,255,.45) for selection; 40px at .25 for modals.

07 · Usage

Rules for new screens

DO

  • Wrap every full screen in scanlines on --background.
  • Set titles, buttons and results in Outfit 900, uppercase Turkish.
  • Use mono 10–11px with 0.1–0.4em tracking for every hint and key prompt.
  • Keep P1 pink-red and P2 cyan in every screen, tag and HUD element.
  • Make each menu work by keyboard first: arrows, Enter, digit shortcuts.

DON'T

  • Use neutral greys. Borders are #272744, panels #121124.
  • Put neon text-shadow on body copy or more than one element per screen.
  • Use pink-red for anything friendly. It means danger, low guard or P1.
  • Round corners past 0.75rem. HUD bars are slanted, never pills.
  • Add new animations before reusing blink, bob, flicker or grid-scroll.
